## Scoring & Passing Standard

- **Passing standard:** **80% overall AND 100% on Part B (Scope of Practice & Safety).**
- Part B items are **critical-safety**: any incorrect answer means the employee reviews **Modules 3 & 8** and re-tests before working independently.
- Suggested weighting: 1 point per numbered item; multi-part short answers score full credit only if all required elements are present (noted below).
