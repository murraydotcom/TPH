# Answer Key *(trainer copy — keep confidential during quizzes)*

### Quiz 1 — Provider / NP
1. **96132 / 96136** (and confirms tier / BHI codes). *(8.1)*
2. Any three: interpretation note (96132), cognitive **diagnosis / ICD-10**, full **care plan**, **weekly target list**, digital plan, follow-up schedule. *(2.6)*
3. ⚠️ Any two: sudden cognitive decline · head injury · stroke-like symptoms · rapid personality change · severe memory loss · loss of orientation. *(4.4)*
4. Any three: CBC · CMP · Vitamin D · B12/Folate/MMA · TSH/Free T4 · A1C · ESR/CRP · homocysteine · hormones. *(4.5 / 7.2)*
5. **The Provider.** *(2.1)*
6. When a **formal diagnosis or educational eligibility** is needed (full diagnostic / neuropsychological / psychoeducational evaluation). *(6J / 10.5)*
7. Any two: sleep apnea · insomnia · iron deficiency · B12/folate · vitamin D · thyroid · insulin resistance · inflammation · perimenopause/menopause · medication side effects. *(7.1)*
8. **Mood impacts cognition.** *(2.6)*
9. **Week 12; 96132.** *(10.1)*
10. Any three: continue · modify tier · maintenance · discharge · refer. *(10.4)*

### Quiz 2 — APD
1. The APD **finds and resolves** every untracked patient, overdue note, missed session, and unassigned referral — **without being told**. *(3.2)*
2. **No referral unassigned > 24 hours.** *(8.3)*
3. The **coordinator's monthly summary — reviewed before the Provider**. *(3.2 / 10.1)*
4. **Weekly.** *(8.3)*
5. **< 75% attendance or 2+ missed sessions.** *(8.3)*
6. ⚠️ **Route to the Provider the same day** (APD routes, Provider decides). *(8.4)*
7. **3 business days.** *(8.3)*
8. **~2 weeks in advance.** *(3.2)*
9. ⚠️ You interpret **trends (non-clinical)**; you never provide **clinical interpretation / a diagnosis**. *(3.2 / 3.6)*
10. **Monthly.** *(8.3)*

### Quiz 3 — Care Coordinator
1. **45–60 minutes.** *(4.2)*
2. Any three: cognitive intake questionnaire · hands-on **Quick Test Kit** · **CogniFit/Creyos** assignment · consents · scoring entry. *(4.2)*
3. **Same day.** *(8.2)*
4. **48 hours** of intake. *(4.2)*
5. **ICD-10 · deficits · interventions · functional response · homework.** *(8.2)*
6. **3+ consecutive non-compliant weeks.** *(8.3)*
7. ⚠️ Don't diagnose — explain these are **screening tools**; the **Provider** reviews and discusses; put it on the **review list**. *(9.2)*
8. **"Baseline review needed."** *(2.5 / 4.2)*
9. On the **intake scoring sheet in Notion**. *(4.2)*
10. **30–45 minutes.** *(2.7)*

### Quiz 4 — MA
1. Intro handout · confirm **device access** · schedule intake · send **referral task** to Care Coordinator. *(3.4)*
2. **Within 7 days** (same day if possible). *(4.2)*
3. **Device access** (phone / tablet / computer for home testing). *(3.4)*
4. Any two: program overview · what to expect · time commitment · cost/insurance info · coordinator contact. *(2.4)*
5. The **Care Coordinator.** *(3.4)*
6. Note it and offer a **virtual/in-clinic option**; flag the coordinator so testing can be arranged. *(2.4)*
7. ⚠️ **False.** *(3.6)*
8. ⚠️ **Tell the APD / Provider** — escalate; the APD routes to the Provider same day. *(8.4)*
9. **Yes**, if time is available. *(2.4)*
10. **Testing setup** or **login troubleshooting.** *(3.4)*

### Quiz 5 — Front Desk
1. Any three: schedule weekly sessions / 12-week series · appointment reminders · attendance compliance · rescheduling · no-show follow-up. *(3.5)*
2. **12 weeks.** *(2.4 / 2.7)*
3. **Weekly.** *(2.7)*
4. **< 75% attendance or 2+ missed sessions** (tell the APD). *(8.3)*
5. **Appointment reminders.** *(3.5)*
6. **Schedule the intake / route to the Care Coordinator** (self/family referral is a valid entry point). *(4.1)*
7. The **APD** (attendance tracking). *(8.3)*
8. ⚠️ **False** — route clinical questions to the coordinator/Provider. *(3.6)*
9. **"CharmED Minds Intake Appointment (60 minutes)."** *(2.4)*
10. Attendance affects **billing compliance and program outcomes.** *(3.5 / 8.3)*
