About The Porter House

We address complex lifestyle challenges with compassion, integrity, and experience.

Our commitment is to deliver on our promises and help every person seek an ELVT’D life — a structured, supportive environment where individuals and families can rebuild, recover, and rise.

01

Our Mission

To provide a structured, supportive, and empowering environment where individuals and families can rebuild, recover, and rise.

02

Our Vision

To become a trusted wellness hub in Baltimore City that connects care, housing, programming, and personal development under one elevated standard.

Our Values

What We Offer

Care, structure, and support under one elevated standard.

01

Behavioral Health Support

Connection to therapy, case management, wellness planning, and partner-based care resources.

02

PHP & Recovery Programming

Daily structure, program attendance support, recovery-centered activities, accountability, and community engagement.

03

Resident support, daily check-ins, house expectations, safety procedures, and stability-focused structure.

Life Skills & Workforce Readiness

Personal development, employment preparation, financial responsibility, grooming, wellness, and leadership-building.
