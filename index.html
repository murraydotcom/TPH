02/04

What We Do

Programs that lift the whole person.

Coordinated, community-based support — each program a step toward a stable, elevated life.

  1. 01

    Behavioral Health Support

    Connection to therapy, case management, wellness planning, and partner-based care resources.

  2. 02

    PHP & Recovery Programming

    Daily structure, program attendance support, recovery-centered activities, accountability, and community engagement.

  3. 03

    Supportive Housing — The Porter House

    Structured transitional housing with resident support, daily check-ins, house expectations, safety procedures, and stability-focused care.

  4. 04

    Life Skills & Workforce Readiness

    Personal development, employment preparation, financial responsibility, grooming, wellness, and leadership-building.

More than services — a movement.

People need structure, opportunity, and a safe place to grow. We bring Baltimore heart to every step.
